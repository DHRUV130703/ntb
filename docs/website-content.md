# IMPORTANT BRAND RULES

1. Never use invented client names, testimonials, awards or performance statistics.
2. Clearly distinguish concept work from commissioned projects.
3. Use actual founder photographs and real work wherever possible.
4. Do not promise guaranteed leads, sales, engagement or automation outcomes.
5. Describe AI services in terms of actual business applications, not vague claims about revolutionising businesses.
6. Every service section should have a clear enquiry link.
7. Keep the website copy confident, human, clear and professional.

The objective is to make visitors understand what we offer, trust how we work and feel confident starting a conversation with us.
